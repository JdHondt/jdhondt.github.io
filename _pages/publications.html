My work sits in two areas. During my PhD at TU Eindhoven I built algorithms for similarity search and correlation discovery on multivariate time series: how to compare series with many channels, and how to find matching subsequences or correlated groups in large collections quickly. At the Barcelona Supercomputing Center I now work on deep-learning downscaling of atmospheric chemistry simulations, with an emphasis on physical constraints and probabilistic models. Each entry below has its own page with the abstract, citation and links to the paper, code and slides.
