A 20-minute talk on multivariate correlation analysis and the [Correlation Detective](https://correlationdetective.com/) library at the Dutch Seminar on Data Systems Design (DSDSD). Compared with the [VLDB talk](/talks/2022-09-09-cd-vldb), this one spends more time on the different correlation measures the [journal version](/publication/cd_journal_2024) supports.

[Watch the recording on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qS0wJAr4Xtc), [download the slides](/files/dsdsd_23_03_2023_20min.pptx) (PowerPoint), or see the [talk page at DSDSD](https://dsdsd.da.cwi.nl/past_talks/Jens-d-Hondt/).
