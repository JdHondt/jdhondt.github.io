At the MulTiSA workshop of ICDE 2024 in Utrecht I presented [Beyond the Dimensions](/publication/icde_jens_talk), our first structured evaluation of distance measures for multivariate time series. The talk introduces the taxonomy of measures (how they treat the channels: independently or together) and shows why no single measure wins. The much larger follow-up study appeared at [SIGMOD 2025](/publication/sigmod_2025).
