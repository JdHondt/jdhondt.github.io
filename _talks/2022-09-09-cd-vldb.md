In this talk I presented [Correlation Detective](https://correlationdetective.com/), our library for multivariate correlation analysis, at VLDB 2022 in Sydney. It goes with the paper [Multivariate correlations discovery in static and streaming data](/publication/cd_conf_2022), written with Koen Minartz and Odysseas Papapetrou. The extended journal version is [here](/publication/cd_journal_2024).

[Download the slides](/files/vldb22_10min.pptx) (PowerPoint), read the [paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.14778/3514061.3514072), or [watch the recording](https://correlationdetective.com/pages/technicalities/).
