A short pitch on our work on automated health nudges: motivational messages that adapt to a person's personality and preferences, tested in a randomised controlled trial across the TU/e campus. The full results are in our [CONTEXT 2019 paper](/publication/context_2019), which I presented a few days later in Trento.

[Download the slides](/files/dss2019_pitch.pdf) (PDF). More about the summit on the [TU/e page](https://www.tue.nl/en/research/research-areas/data-science/data-science-summit-2019).
