I presented our paper [Evaluation of computer-tailored motivational messaging in a health promotion context](/publication/context_2019) at [CONTEXT 2019](http://context19.di.unito.it/) in Trento. The talk covers the adaptive persuasive system we built, the randomised controlled trial, and the main finding: tailoring improves how people feel about the messages, but not necessarily their behaviour months later.
